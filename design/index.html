Round 2: modelled on popular chat apps

Centered column

like ChatGPT

A chat list on the left and one calm reading column. Your question sits in a pill on the right, the answer is plain text, and the lookup steps fold into one line.

Answer page

like Perplexity

Each question becomes a page: the question as a headline, source cards first, a citation chip on every claim, and related questions to continue.

Messenger

like WhatsApp or iMessage

Bubbles, an avatar and timestamps in a compact window. Articles arrive as link previews and suggested replies sit above the input.

Launchpad

like Gemini or Copilot

No sidebar. Opens on a large greeting with starter cards, then a roomy conversation with a source chip under each answer.

Thread

like Slack or Discord

Dark and dense. Named message rows with times, the lookup steps as a checked list, and each article as an unfurled link.

Round 1: earlier directions (Telescope is the current app)

Reading Room

serif · paper · wikipedia blue

Answers set like encyclopedia entries, with index-card tool steps and a Sources colophon. Quiet, slower, bookish.

Telescope

dark console · observatory log

Sessions rail plus a traced tool log beside the answer; the agent's work is an instrument reading, not an abstraction.

Inkwell

minimal · superscript footnotes

ChatGPT-shape but no bubbles — a paper's reference apparatus: superscript [1][2] resolved by an expandable References list.

Atlas

two-pane · live bibliography index

Conversation left, a card index of cited articles fills in as the agent works. The sources become a first-class surface.
