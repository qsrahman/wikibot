Who discovered penicillin?

Penicillin was discovered in September 1928, when Alexander Fleming[1] found an accidental mould on a culture plate had destroyed the surrounding staphylococci. He named the substance penicillin, but it was Howard Florey and Ernst Chain at Oxford[3] who purified it into a medicine, and the three shared the 1945 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine.
