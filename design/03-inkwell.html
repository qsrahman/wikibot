Who discovered penicillin?

Penicillin was discovered in September 1928, when Alexander Fleming1 found a stray mould on a culture plate had destroyed the surrounding staphylococci. He named the substance penicillin, but turning it into a medicine fell to Howard Florey and Ernst Chain at Oxford2; the three shared the 1945 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine.
